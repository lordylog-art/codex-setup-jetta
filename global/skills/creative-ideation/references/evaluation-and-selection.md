# Evaluation And Selection

Use this reference when the user wants prioritization, ranking, filtering, or a shortlist.

## Quick Filters

Use a fast pass for small tasks:

- `Strong`: distinctive and useful
- `Maybe`: promising but underdeveloped
- `Weak`: generic, crowded, or hard to explain

## Simple Scorecard

Score from 1 to 5 across:

- originality
- fit to objective
- feasibility
- speed to test
- upside

Use equal weights unless the user signals a different priority.

## Strategic Selection Lenses

Choose the lens that matches the request:

- `Best immediate win`: highest feasibility with clear value
- `Best asymmetric bet`: larger upside with acceptable risk
- `Best brand builder`: strongest memorability and positioning impact
- `Best learning vehicle`: fastest way to discover what the market wants
- `Best portfolio mix`: one safe, one balanced, one bold

## Refinement Moves

When an idea is almost good enough, improve it by:

- narrowing the audience
- simplifying the mechanism
- making the payoff more concrete
- adding a stronger distribution hook
- changing the packaging or business model
- increasing emotional contrast

## Output Guidance

When ranking ideas, explain the winner in plain language. The user should understand not only which option wins, but why it deserves attention now.
